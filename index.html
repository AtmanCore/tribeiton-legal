Tribe-It-On

Walk together, grow together.

Gentle step challenges for your tribe: family and closest friends. Private, invite-only, and kind.

What it does

  • Make a private tribe and invite the people you care about with a link.
  • Set a daily step goal and walk toward shared goals together.
  • Celebrate birthdays: walk for a tribe member and write them a message they open on the day.

Works with your tracker

Tribe-It-On reads your step count from Health Connect on Android, Apple Health on iPhone, WHOOP, or Fitbit and Pixel Watch. It only reads activity data, never writes it, and you can disconnect any time.
