Tribe-It-On privacy policy

Effective October 23, 2026

Tribe-It-On lets families and friends share daily step counts and goals in private, invite-only tribes.

What we collect

  • Account: your name, email address and profile photo from the sign-in provider you choose.
  • Activity totals: daily step counts, and daily active or workout minutes, read with your permission from Health Connect (Android), Apple Health (iOS), Fitbit / Google Health or WHOOP. We store one total per day. We never collect location, heart rate, or individual workouts or sessions.
  • What you add: tribe names, goals, messages and scrapbook posts.
  • Date of birth: asked once. Your tribes see only the month and day, so they can celebrate your birthday; the year stays private to you.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, a device token from Firebase Cloud Messaging so we can send reminders and tribe updates. Some notifications include step counts: your own (for example how many steps are left to your daily goal), or a tribe member's when they share their steps in full. You can switch each kind off in the app, or turn notifications off on your phone.

Who can use it

Tribe-It-On is for people aged 13 and older. We ask your date of birth when you sign up.

How we use it

Only to show leaderboards, goals and messages to the members of tribes you joined, subject to your visibility setting (full, rank only or hidden). We do not sell data, show ads, or share health data with third parties, apart from the service providers that run the app's features for us: Google Cloud runs the app, Neon (on Amazon Web Services in the US) hosts the database, and notification text (which can include step counts, as described above) is delivered through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service. They process it only to provide that service. Health data is never used for advertising or credit decisions.

Health Connect and Apple Health

Use of information received from Health Connect and HealthKit follows the Health Connect Permissions policy and Apple's HealthKit guidelines, including their Limited Use requirements. We only read data, and you can revoke access at any time in Health Connect or the Health app.

Storage and security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Fitbit and WHOOP access tokens are encrypted at rest. Disconnecting a source stops all syncing from it.

Deleting your data

Delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete account) or see account deletion.
