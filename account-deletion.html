Delete your Tribe-It-On account

In the app

  1. Open Tribe-It-On and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

By email

If you no longer have the app, email tribeiton.app@gmail.com from your account's email address. We delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

Deletion is immediate and permanent. It removes your profile details, all synced activity data, Fitbit/WHOOP connections, sign-in identities, and the messages and scrapbook posts you wrote. You leave all tribes. Past tribe results keep an anonymous "Former member" entry with no name or contact details.
